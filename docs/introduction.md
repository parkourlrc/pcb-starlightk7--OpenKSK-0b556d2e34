# Introduction

Welcome to OpenKSK!

This is an Open Source Hardware (OSHW) project aimed at creating 1:1 reproduction parts Sony KSK series of CD-ROM laser boards. These boards are used in retro gaming consoles such as the Sega CD Model (KSK-1200A), Sega CD Model 2 (KSK-1310A), and Neo Geo CD Frontloader (KSK-1230A).

The initial release of the project is focused on the KSK-1200A.

# Why Would I Want This?

There are many reasons why you might want an OpenKSK part

* Repairing Damage to Original Boards
  * These boards have notoriously fragile capcitor pads and are often damaged during recap. 
    * They are single sided eyelets with no plating, attached by weak glue and in many cases, a single thin trace with no teardrop.
    * OpenKSK boards offer a mechanism for clean repair, complete with stronger pads.
  * Boards can also be damaged by a variety of incidents
    * Leaked Capacitors
    * Liquid Spills
    * Flooded basements/warehouses
    * Humidty damage from improper storage
    * Failed mod attempts
  * These parts can serve as replacement parts to repair the unit.
* Learning
  * You may want to learn about how the drive system works
  * The ability to build one by hand is an excellent way to gain some insight into how a CD-ROM drive works
* Customization
  * Some people find joy in customizing a beat-up unit while restoring it, and OSHW parts are great for that. 
  * Please be responsible and choose a rustic unit for your restoration so that we don't needlessly deplete the supply of original units in good condition
* Integrating fixes/mods
  * The reference board is a 1:1 reproduction, but you may want to customize it.
  * You may want to remix a custom board that integrates mods or fixes of your choice into the board.
  * Anyone can create a derivative of the PCB under the terms of the [CERN-OHL-S](https://ohwr.org/cern_ohl_s_v2.txt) license.
